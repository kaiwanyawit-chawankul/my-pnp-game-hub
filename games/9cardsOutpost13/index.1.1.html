OUTPOST 13: LOOP PROTOCOL

Phase 1 Sci-Fi Horror Micro Game — Components, Rules & Print-and-Play Cards

Components List

To play Outpost 13: Loop Protocol, you will need the following physical components:

  • 9 Cards (Provided Below):
    • 7 Location Cards (Draft 6 from the 7 Locations pool)
    • 1 Time & Tracker Card (Contains Time Track, Winning Condition & Loop Reset Rules)
    • 1 Operatives Dossier Card (Characters)
  • 4 Operative Tokens: Representing Sgt. Vance, Pvt. Miller, Rookie Chen, and Pvt. Aris. (Double-sided normal / wounded state).
  • Threat Markers: Small cubes or tokens (approx. 10–12 pieces) to track threat levels on locations and the time tracker.
  • Action Dice: Standard 6-sided dice (D6) for threat rolls and operative mission checks.

Game Rules & Phase Structure

Core Objective

Pvt. Miller is trapped in a repeating 3-minute time loop. Secure 3 Directives by clearing location cards before Time reaches 10, with no dead operatives.

Turn Phase Breakdown

  1. Threat Activation: Roll 1 D6 to add 1 Threat Token to the matching location. (Skips any location that is already Face-Down!)
  2. Assign Operatives: Place characters (Vance, Miller, Chen, Aris) onto locations to clear threats and complete missions. Multiple operatives can team up on a single location.
  3. Mission Checks: Roll D6 vs Location Target:
    • Pass 1st Time: Card is Tapped (1st Check complete).
    • Pass 2nd Time: Card flips Face-Down (Secures 1 Directive, immune to future threats).
    • Fail Roll: Operative suffers the location's specific Hazard effect.
  4. End of Turn: Unresolved active threats trigger their end-of-turn effects. Time advances by the total number of active threat tokens across all locations.
  5. Win / Loss Condition: Secure 3 Directives with no dead operatives before Time reaches 10. Defeat occurs if Time reaches 10, any location reaches 3 Threats (overrun), or Miller dies twice.

Miller's Loop Reset

If Pvt. Miller dies for any reason, the time loop collapses! Flip the Time & Tracker Card to represent the reset into Loop 2: Time resets back to 0, all operatives are revived to normal status, and previously cleared Directives are retained. If Miller dies a second time, the game ends in defeat.
