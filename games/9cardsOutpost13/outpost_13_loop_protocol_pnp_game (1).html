1. PHASE 1 NARRATIVE & SETTING

Outpost 13 sits perched on an ash-choked ridge surrounded by razor wire and dead pine forest in the Rimlands apocalyptic wasteland. The horizon is locked under a 72-hour total solar eclipse bordered by a ring of dark violet fire. Deep beneath the concrete bunker, a subterranean 4.2 Hertz biological signal pulses, driving hordes of skinless abominations, Crawlers, and acoustic Screaming Stalkers toward the Sector 4 breach point.

2. PHASE 1 SQUAD DOSSIERS & ABILITIES

Tactical Sgt. Vance:

Reincarnated Tokyo corporate clerk endowed with extreme physical strength, super reflexes, real-time ballistic trajectory predictions, and recoil elimination via his System HUD over his missing eye.

Pvt. Miller:

Battle-hardened Time-Loop Veteran who predicts monster spawn points and hazard threats from past dead lifetimes. When Miller dies, time resets to 3 minutes pre-breach.

Rookie Chen:

Radio technician experiencing awakening 4.2Hz psychic sensitivity. Can feel biological pulses before attacks occur, granting luck manipulation and hazard rerolls.

Pvt. Aris:

Greenhorn recruit clutching a canteen and tape recorder. Serves as Squad Record Keeper & Logistics Support, managing ammo supplies, rations, and survival directives.

3. GAMEPLAY TURN PHASES

  • Phase 1: Worker Allocation — Place your 4 operative tokens across the 6 Outpost 13 locations.
  • Phase 2: Hazard Checks — Roll 1d6 per assigned location. Apply Vance speed bonuses, Miller foresight, or Chen luck rerolls.
  • Phase 3: Directives Resolution — Check off completed survival directives (e.g., deadbolt clicks, vacuum equalization).
  • Phase 4: 4.2Hz Hive Telemetry Surge — Advance Blood Moon Threat tracker. Reset game if Miller dies.

4. WIN & LOSS CONDITIONS

ZERO CASUALTIES VICTORY: • Complete 3 Sector 4 Directives.
• Secure Sector 4 Gate with 0 squad deaths.
DEFEAT CONDITIONS: • Blood Moon Threat reaches 10.
• Miller dies in Loop 2.
• All 4 squad members killed.
