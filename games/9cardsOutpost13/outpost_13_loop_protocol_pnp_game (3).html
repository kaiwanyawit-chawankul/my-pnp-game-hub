Single-Player & Co-op Survival Card Game

Survive the Loop. Contain the Breach.

Outpost 13 is trapped in a temporal feedback loop infested with mutating anomalies. As chief commander, manage your squad of heroes, deploy limited energy dice, harvest essential power cells, and defeat the Anomaly Boss before Turn 10 ends.

🔄 Round Sequence (5 Phases)

1

Roll & Calculate Distance

Anglers/Commanders roll 1 die from the shared pool simultaneously. Choose a target sector 1 to 6 positions down the sector stack. Available Power Dice = Roll Value - Target Distance You may bank unused dice to raise your Power Level (+1 Power Level per banked die, max 5).

2

Deploy Heroes & Place Tokens

Simultaneously assign hero tokens onto sector target cards. You may spread deployment across sectors or stack up to 3 tokens on one sector to focus firepower.

3

Sector Competition & Priority

If multiple heroes enter the same sector card, determine priority:

  • Trait Match: Hero matching sector affinity acts first.
  • Roll-Off: If tied, roll-off using shared dice. Higher total takes priority. Spend 1 Power Level for +1 extra die.

4

Execute Sector Checks

Resolve checks sequentially using assigned Power Dice. Target number equals Sector Defense (or Sector Defense + 1 if unmatched hero). ✓ Success: Secure sector reward & regain hero token free. ✗ Failure: Sector anomaly flees to bottom of deck. Pay 1 Power Level to recover token.

5

Anomaly Boss Encounter

When the Boss Anomaly is revealed, players combine dice to strike. Boss Damage = Total Dice Score - Boss Armor Reduce Boss HP tracker. If HP reaches 0, the loop breaks instantly and you WIN!

⚡ Quick Mechanics

Action Cost / Requirement
Power Dice Roll Value − Target Distance
Target Roll Sector Def (+1 if no match)
Extra Die Spend 1 Power Level (↓)
Bank Die +1 Power Level (Max 5)
Token Recovery Free on Win / 1 Power on Fail

🌲 Sector D6 Encounter Table

D6 Roll 1: Hazard Trap (Search Check)
D6 Roll 2-3: Hostile Mutation / Monster
D6 Roll 4-5: Resource Cell / Supplies
D6 Roll 6: Rare Tech Artifact

🏆 Victory & Scoring

The game ends immediately when the Boss Anomaly HP drops to 0. Calculate final score:

Score = Sector VP + Boss VP + Unused Power Cells
