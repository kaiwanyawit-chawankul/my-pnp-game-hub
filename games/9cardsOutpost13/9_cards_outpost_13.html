OUTPOST 13: LOOP PROTOCOL - OFFICIAL RULEBOOK

A 9-Card Tactical Sci-Fi Loop Survival Game (Solo / Co-op)

1. Mission Overview

Miller and his specialized operative team are trapped in a decaying temporal loop inside Outpost 13. Parasitic vines, breach anomalies, and psychic pulse signals threaten to collapse the facility. To break the loop, Miller's team must secure 3 Directives before station corruption (Time Track) reaches 10.

2. Game Phase Sequence

  1. Threat Activation: Roll 1 D6. Add 1 Threat token to the location matching the roll number. Note: Face-down (cleared) locations are skipped; if a cleared location is rolled, no threat is placed!
  2. Assign Operatives: Place your available Operatives onto active locations to attempt missions or clear threats.
  3. Mission Checks: Roll a D6 against the location's Target Number (apply operative bonuses).
    • 1st Success: Tap the location card (1 Directive Click secured).
    • 2nd Success: Flip the card Face-Down (Directive Fully Secured!).
    • Failure: Suffer the location's Hazard penalty.
  4. End of Turn: Unresolved threats trigger their negative effects. Time advances by the total number of active Threat tokens currently on the board.
  5. Win Condition: Secure 3 Directives (flip 3 location cards face-down) before Time reaches 10.

3. Operative Dossier Abilities

VANCE (System HUD)

Grants +2 Strength modifier on all combat & mission check rolls.

MILLER (Loop Veteran)

Allows 1 reroll per turn. If Miller is sacrificed at Sector 4, the entire loop resets (Time -> 0, Team Healed, Cleared Directives remain!).

CHEN (4.2Hz Psychic)

+1 modifier to friendly roll results in adjacent sectors.

ARIS (Record Keeper)

Permanently reduces Mission Target Difficulty by 1 at her deployed location.
