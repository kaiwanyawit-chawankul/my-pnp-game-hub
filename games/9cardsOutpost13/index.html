OUTPOST 13: LOOP PROTOCOL

Phase 1 Sci-Fi Horror Micro Game — Components, Rules & Print-and-Play Cards

Components List

To play Outpost 13: Loop Protocol, you will need the following physical components:

  • 9 Cards (Provided Below):
    • 7 Location Cards (Draw 6 of the 7 to play)
    • 1 Time & Tracker Card (Time Track, Winning Condition & Loop Reset Rules)
    • 1 Operatives Dossier Card (Characters)
  • 4 Operative Tokens: Sgt. Vance, Pvt. Miller, Rookie Chen, Pvt. Aris (double-sided: normal / wounded). Printable on the last page: cut out each pair and glue back to back.
  • Threat Markers: 12 small cubes or tokens (printable on the last page). A location can never hold more than 2.
  • Time Marker: Any coin or token, placed on the Time Track printed on the Time & Tracker Card.
  • Action Dice: Standard 6-sided dice (D6) for threat rolls and mission checks.

Game Rules & Phase Structure

Core Objective

Pvt. Miller is trapped in a repeating 3-minute time loop. Secure 3 Directives (each Face-Down location is 1 Directive) by completing location missions before Time reaches 10, with all 4 operatives alive at the end.

Setup

Shuffle the 7 Location cards and draw 6. Lay them in a row and number them 1 to 6 in draw order. The 7th card is not used this game. Time starts at 0: put the Time marker on 0 of the Time & Tracker Card. Place all 4 operatives in the middle of the table. Start with the Time & Tracker Card Loop 1 side up.

Turn Phase Breakdown

  1. Threat Activation: Roll 1 D6 and add 1 Threat Token to the location with that number. If that location is Face-Down, nothing happens (no token, no reroll).
  2. Assign Operatives: Place living operatives onto locations. Any number of operatives may team up on one location; each makes their own Mission Check.
  3. Mission Checks: Each operative rolls a D6 vs the Location Target:
    • Pass: The mission is progressed and all Threat Tokens on that location are cleared. If the card lists a Reward, you gain it (once per card per turn, except Medical healing on each pass).
    • 1st Pass: Card is Tapped.
    • 2nd Pass: Card flips Face-Down (secures 1 Directive, no more threats there). With a team-up, both passes can happen in the same turn.
    • Fail: That operative suffers the location's Hazard.
  4. End of Turn: Remaining Threat Tokens trigger their location's Threat effect. Time advances by the total number of Threat Tokens left on all locations.
  5. Win / Loss: Win when 3 Directives are secured and all operatives are alive. Defeat if Time reaches 10, any location reaches 3 Threats (overrun), Miller dies a second time, or any operative dies in Loop 2 (there is no more reset to revive them).

Wounds & Death

The 1st Wound flips an operative to its wounded side. The 2nd Wound kills them. A dead operative cannot be assigned or help. Healing flips a wounded operative back to normal. An Entangled operative must stay on the same location next turn; teammates can still join them. A Loop Reset frees everyone.

Miller's Loop Reset

If Pvt. Miller dies for any reason, the loop collapses and Loop 2 begins: flip the Time & Tracker Card, set Time to 0, remove all Threat Tokens, and revive all operatives to normal. Location cards stay as they are (Tapped and Face-Down cards keep their progress). Miller may die deliberately in Loop 1 as a strategy. If Miller dies a second time, the game is lost. In Loop 2 there is no second reset: any operative death is an immediate defeat, so keep everyone alive and use Medical & Decon to heal.

Clarifications

  • Win check: at the end of the Mission Checks phase, as soon as the 3rd Directive is secured with all 4 operatives alive.
  • Operative abilities: Vance +2 on his own roll. Miller rerolls one of his own rolls per turn. Chen gives +1 to one teammate's roll on the same location per turn. Aris lowers the Target by 1 on his own roll.
  • Threat effects resolve at end of turn, in location order 1 to 6, for every Threat Token still on a face-up location, before Time is counted. An effect that adds tokens can cause an overrun.
  • Overrun: a location that would hold 3 Threat Tokens is an immediate defeat. Sub-Basement Drainage adds 2 with its effect, so a token left there at end of turn is an overrun. Clear it the turn it appears.
  • Air Duct: while it holds a Threat Token, Mission Checks on other locations do not count.
  • Medical Door Jam: tokens here cannot be cleared. They count for Time and are removed at end of turn.
  • Face-Down cards never receive tokens and are skipped by clockwise moves. A Face-Down Medical card still heals.
