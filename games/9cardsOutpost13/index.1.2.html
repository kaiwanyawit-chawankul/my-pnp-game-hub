OUTPOST 13: LOOP PROTOCOL

Phase 1 Sci-Fi Horror Micro Game — Components, Rules & Print-and-Play Cards

Components List

To play Outpost 13: Loop Protocol, you will need the following physical components:

  • 9 Cards (Provided Below):
    • 7 Location Cards (Draw 6 of the 7 to play)
    • 1 Time & Tracker Card (Time Track, Winning Condition & Loop Reset Rules)
    • 1 Operatives Dossier Card (Characters)
  • 4 Operative Tokens: Sgt. Vance, Pvt. Miller, Rookie Chen, Pvt. Aris (double-sided: normal / wounded).
  • Threat Markers: Small cubes or tokens (approx. 10–12 pieces).
  • Action Dice: Standard 6-sided dice (D6) for threat rolls and mission checks.

Game Rules & Phase Structure

Core Objective

Pvt. Miller is trapped in a repeating 3-minute time loop. Secure 3 Directives by completing location missions before Time reaches 10, with all 4 operatives alive at the end.

Setup

Shuffle the 7 Location cards and draw 6. Lay them in a row and number them 1 to 6 in draw order. The 7th card is not used this game. Time starts at 0.

Turn Phase Breakdown

  1. Threat Activation: Roll 1 D6 and add 1 Threat Token to the location with that number. If that location is Face-Down, nothing happens (no token, no reroll).
  2. Assign Operatives: Place living operatives onto locations. Any number of operatives may team up on one location; each makes their own Mission Check.
  3. Mission Checks: Each operative rolls a D6 vs the Location Target:
    • Pass: The mission is progressed and all Threat Tokens on that location are cleared.
    • 1st Pass: Card is Tapped.
    • 2nd Pass: Card flips Face-Down (secures 1 Directive, no more threats there). With a team-up, both passes can happen in the same turn.
    • Fail: That operative suffers the location's Hazard.
  4. End of Turn: Remaining Threat Tokens trigger their location's Threat effect. Time advances by the total number of Threat Tokens left on all locations.
  5. Win / Loss: Win when 3 Directives are secured and all operatives are alive. Defeat if Time reaches 10, any location reaches 3 Threats (overrun), or Miller dies a second time.

Wounds & Death

The 1st Wound flips an operative to its wounded side. The 2nd Wound kills them. A dead operative cannot be assigned or help. Healing flips a wounded operative back to normal.

Miller's Loop Reset

If Pvt. Miller dies for any reason, the loop collapses and Loop 2 begins: flip the Time & Tracker Card, set Time to 0, remove all Threat Tokens, and revive all operatives to normal. Location cards stay as they are (Tapped and Face-Down cards keep their progress). Miller may die deliberately in Loop 1 as a strategy. If Miller dies a second time, the game is lost. In Loop 2 there is no second reset, so keep everyone alive and use Medical & Decon to heal.
