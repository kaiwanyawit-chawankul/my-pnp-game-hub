1. THE RIMLANDS ANOMALY & WORLD LORE

Built into an ash-choked ridge surrounded by dead pine trees, Outpost 13 is a concrete bunker fortress hiding a clandestine bio-weapons laboratory engineered by Aethelgard Bio-Pharmaceuticals under Dr. Victor Sterling. When the planetary Blood Moon Eclipse locks in dark violet fire, an organic bio-refinery in the Central Drainage Vault broadcasts a subterranean 4.2 Hertz telemetry signal to feed an adaptive parasitic hive network.

2. OPERATIVES DOSSIERS & ACTIVE ABILITIES

Tactical Sgt. Vance:

Reincarnated senior salaryman from Tokyo. Semi-translucent System HUD grants Inventory Inspection and Overtime Surge (cancels weapon recoil & rerolls hazard checks).

Pvt. Miller:

Time-Loop Veteran trapped in a repeating death cycle resetting 3 minutes before the breach. When Miller dies, the entire game resets to Loop 2 retaining earned Loop Intel.

Pvt. Aris (Specimen Zero):

Genetic clone of Dr. Victor Sterling holding an audio cassette. His biometric neural match allows him to override Sterling's central telemetry console at Location 06.

Rookie Chen:

Communications tech with latent Psychic Sensitivity. Physically feels the 4.2Hz telemetry signal before attacks, providing tactical hazard warnings.

3. GAMEPLAY TURN SEQUENCE

  • Phase 1: Worker Allocation — Station your 4 operatives across available locations.
  • Phase 2: Hazard Checks — Roll 1d6 for each location. Apply Vance HUD modifiers or Aris console overrides.
  • Phase 3: Survival Directives — Complete tasks (e.g. equalize vacuum, check deadbolts) to clear objectives.
  • Phase 4: 4.2Hz Hive Telemetry Surge — Threat advances by +1 (+ Biomass level). Check for Miller death time reset.
