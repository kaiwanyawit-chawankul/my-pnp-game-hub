The Midnight Catch — Print & Play

📋 How to Setup

  • Cut out the 9 cards from Sheet 1 and mix them up face-down on the table.
  • Cut out the 2 Jar Cards below—give one to each player.
  • Cut out the round tokens and place them in a common pool.

🎮 How to Play (4-5 Years Old)

  • Take turns flipping **one** card face-up.
  • **Flip a Firefly:** Put 1 token inside your jar!
  • **Flip a Flashlight, Bulb, or Lamp:** Scared! 0 tokens.
  • Play until all cards are flipped. Count tokens, then shuffle for the next round. Best out of 3 or 5 rounds wins!
