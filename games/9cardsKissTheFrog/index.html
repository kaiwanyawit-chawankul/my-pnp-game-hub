Kiss the Frog 💄🐸 v2

Fairy-Tale Memory & Take-That Party Game • 2–4 Players • ~15 min

Page 1 of 4: Rulebook
👑

The Great Wasted Witch & Drunk Prince Incident

At the annual Royal Grand Gala, the Wicked Witch got fed up with the deafening karaoke music and cast a devastating Frog Curse on the Royal Prince! However, the Prince was way too drunk. Instead of panicking, he stumbled around sloppy-kissing EVERYONE—Princesses, wolves, pigs, magical beings, and party guests! Now, all the Princesses have been turned into frogs! There is only ONE True Frog Prince in the pond. Every Princess must scramble through the magical pond to find him, kiss him with true love (or total desperation), reverse the curse, and live Happily Ever After... before they get stuck eating flies forever!

Setup (2–4 Players)

  • Cut out the 9 Pond Cards (Page 2) & 4 Tokens (Page 4).
  • Everyone starts in Princess Mode 👸 with 0 cards in hand.
  • Shuffle the 9 cards and lay them face-down in a 3×3 grid (The Pond).
  • First player: whoever kissed someone most recently. The second player gets 1 extra Flip on their first turn.
🖐️ Hand Limit: 2 Cards! Ending your turn with 2? Play one, or put one back face-down in an empty slot.

Winning the Game

Kiss the True Frog Prince TWICE:

1️⃣ 1st Kiss (As Princess 👸): Flip the Prince face-up → turn your token to Frog Mode 🐸! Then the Shell Game hides him (see below).

2️⃣ 2nd Kiss (As Frog 🐸): Flip the Prince again → YOU WIN IMMEDIATELY! 🏆 Frog = one kiss from winning. Everyone will try to knock you back.

On Your Turn (Spend 2 Actions) Any mix, repeats allowed

Flipped cards are public: remember where everything is!

1. FLIP

Turn 1 Pond card face-up for all to see. Keep it in hand (max 2), or flip it back face-down in the same slot.

2. PLAY

Cast a card from your hand, then place it face-down in any empty Pond slot (the Witch is banished instead).

3. SCRAMBLE

Swap any 2 Pond cards face-down while everyone watches, to ruin the leader's memory!

🐚 The Shell Game: after any kiss, turn the Prince face-down and swap him with other Pond cards 5 times, in the open, while everyone tries to track him. Then the next player's turn begins.

Card Abilities Cheat Sheet (9 Cards Total)

👑 Prince: Flip = kiss. Princess → Frog + Shell Game. Frog → WIN
🧞 Genie: A Princess swaps tokens with any Frog
🔱 Triton: Hands go face-down into Pond, then swap 3 pairs blind
🧚‍♀️ Fairy: Turn any Frog back into a Princess
🐺 Wolf: Steal 1 random card from an opponent
🧙‍♀️ Witch: Banish a Pond card or a card from a hand. Not the Prince. Then banish the Witch
🪞 Mirror: Privately peek at 2 Pond cards
🍎 Apple: Target player skips next turn
🐷 Pig: Everyone passes 1 card of their choice left 👈
