Tabloid Journalism Micro-Euro

GET THE SCOOP. DON'T DEBUNK THE MYTH!

You are rival field photojournalists investigating cryptid sightings. Place your Camera Shot Tokens (1–5) to capture evidence. Gather enough exposure to score front-page headlines, but over-expose or zoom in too close and you'll debunk the myth as a floating tree trunk, a barn owl, or a drafty shutter!

📦 Game Components

  • • Location Deck: 9 Location Cards
  • • Tabloid Roles: 6 Unique Roles
  • • Shot Tokens: 5 per Player (1 to 5)
  • • Scaling Rule: +2 Bust Limit per extra player
  • • Player Count: 2 - 4 Tabloids
  • • Play Time: 15 - 20 Minutes

⚡ How to Play

1. Take Shots (Placement)

Taking turns, players secretly place one face-down Camera Shot Token (1–5) under any of the 3 active Location Cards. Repeat until each player has committed 3 tokens.

2. Dynamic Bust Scaling

Base Bust Limits are printed for 2 players. Add +2 to the Bust Limit for 3 players, and +4 for 4 players to keep higher player counts balanced and strategic!

3. Three Possible Location Outcomes

  • Below Min Requirement: Photo is dark or blurry. No story is printed!
  • Sweet Spot (Min to Bust Limit - 1): SCOOP PUBLISHED! Highest single Shot Token wins 1st Place VP. 2nd highest wins 2nd Place VP.
  • At or Above Scaled Bust Limit: MYTH BUSTED! Too clear! Proves the cryptid is just a tree trunk, owl, or draft. The single highest contributor loses -2 VP.

🧠 Playstyle Dynamics & Strategies

🔥 Aggressive Hounds

Players commit heavy zoom shots (4s and 5s) to guarantee the 1st place scoop. High reward, but if rivals also push hard, the myth instantly busts!

🛡️ Cautious Interns

Players commit modest shots (1s, 2s, 3s) to keep exposure safely in the sweet spot. Very stable, but vulnerable to being starved of minimum required exposure.

🕵️ Mind-Game Saboteurs

Noticing an opponent played a heavy token, a saboteur intentionally drops a high token to force a Bust, penalizing the rival -2 VP!

Micro Euro Cryptid Journalism Game • Complete Field Guide

PLAYERS: 2 – 4
PLAYTIME: 15 – 20 MINS

I. Game Overview

You are rival photojournalists investigating bizarre cryptid sightings. Your goal is to capture sufficient photo evidence for front-page headlines without over-investigating: if you look too closely, you debunk the myth as mundane!

II. Setup

  1. Shuffle the 9 Location Cards and place 3 active locations face-up in the center.
  2. Each player selects 1 Tabloid Role Card and receives 5 Camera Shot Tokens (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
  3. Place Prestige VP tokens in a central bank.

III. Gameplay Structure (3 Rounds)

  • Phase 1 (Taking Shots): Players take turns placing 1 face-down Shot Token under any active location until each player has assigned 3 tokens.
  • Phase 2 (Exposure Resolution): Flip all tokens under each location and sum their values.

IV. Dynamic Scaling Rule

• 2 Players: Use Base Bust Limit.
• 3 Players: Base Limit + 2.
• 4 Players: Base Limit + 4.

V. Resolving Locations

  • Below Min Requirement: Exposure too dark/blurry. Story cannot be printed!
  • Sweet Spot (Min to Scaled Bust Limit - 1): SCOOP PUBLISHED!
    • Highest single Shot Token wins 1st Place Scoop VP.
    • 2nd highest single Shot Token wins 2nd Place Scoop VP.
  • At or Above Scaled Bust Limit: MYTH BUSTED! The photo was over-exposed, proving the cryptid is mundane! The single highest contributor loses -2 VP.

VI. Playstyle Scenarios

• Aggressive Hounds: Pushing high tokens (4s/5s) risks triggering busts, but captures 1st place when successful.

• Cautious Interns: Playing low tokens (1s/2s) stays safe, but risks failing min requirements.

• Saboteurs: Adding tokens to opponent locations to deliberately exceed the Bust Limit and cause -2 VP penalties.

VII. Victory

After 3 rounds (investigating all 9 locations), the journalist with the most Prestige Victory Points wins!
