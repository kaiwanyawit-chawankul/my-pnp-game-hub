Micro Euro Cryptid Journalism Game • 9 Location Cards

Official Field Guide

GET THE SCOOP. DON'T BUST THE MYTH.

You are a field journalist working for competing paranormal tabloids. Across the country, bizarre occurrences are being reported! Your job is to rush to locations, take photographs, and publish sensational articles. But beware: investigate too heavily, and you'll discover the mundane reality (a tree trunk, a weather balloon, a feral dog). If you bust the myth, nobody gets the scoop!

📦 Components

  • • Location Cards: 9 Cards
  • • Journalist Roles: 6 Cards
  • • Player Tokens: 5 per player (1, 2, 3, 4, 5)
  • • Players Supported: 2 - 4 Tabloids
  • • Game Duration: 15 - 20 Mins
Printing Tip: Print the PnP tab on heavy cardstock (250gsm+). Use sleeves for the smooth Euro-game feel!

🎮 How To Play

1 Setup Assignment

Shuffle the 9 Location Cards. Reveal 3 active locations in the middle of the table. Each player picks 1 Role Card and takes 5 Bidding Tokens (numbered 1 through 5 in their color).

2 Secret Investigation (Bidding)

Taking turns, players place one token face-down under any of the 3 active locations. Continue until each player has assigned 3 tokens total.

3 Reveal & Resolution

Flip all placed tokens at each location and sum the values:

  • Below Min Requirement: Not enough evidence gathered. No scoop published.
  • Sweet Spot (Min to Bust - 1): SCOOP PUBLISHED! Highest single contributor earns 1st Place Scoop points; 2nd highest earns 2nd Place.
  • At or Above Bust Limit: MYTH BUSTED! You over-investigated and proved it's mundane (e.g. tree trunk). The contributor of the highest token loses 2 points!

4 Game End

Repeat for 3 full rounds (using all 9 locations). The journalist/tabloid with the highest total Prestige points wins!
