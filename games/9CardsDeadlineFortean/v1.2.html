You are competing field photojournalists sent to investigate cryptid sightings across the country. Place your Camera Shot Tokens (1–5) to capture evidence. Gather enough exposure to score big points on the front page, but over-expose or zoom in too close and you'll debunk the myth as a floating tree trunk, a barn owl, or a drafty shutter!

⚡ Gameplay Summary

1. Take Shots (Placement)

Taking turns, players secretly place one face-down Camera Shot Token (1–5) under any of the 3 active Location Cards. Repeat until each player has assigned 3 Shot Tokens.

2. Reveal & Exposure Check

Flip all tokens under each location and add their total values to calculate total exposure.

3. Three Possible Location Outcomes

  • Below Min Req: Image too dark or blurry. No story is published!
  • Sweet Spot (Min to Bust Limit - 1): SCOOP PUBLISHED! Player with the highest single Shot Token claims 1st Place VP. 2nd highest claims 2nd Place VP.
  • At or Above Bust Limit: MYTH BUSTED! The image is too clear, proving the cryptid is just a mundane object! The single highest contributor loses -2 VP.

DEADLINE: FORTEAN

Micro Euro Cryptid Journalism Game • Field Rulebook

PLAYERS: 2 – 4
TIME: 15 – 20 MINS

I. Objective & Concept

You are rival photojournalists sent to investigate bizarre sightings across the country. Your goal is to get high-exposure photos for front-page headlines without over-investigating: if you look too closely, you debunk the myth as mundane!

II. Setup

  1. Shuffle the 9 Location Cards and place 3 active locations face-up in the center.
  2. Each player chooses 1 Tabloid Role Card and receives 5 Camera Shot Tokens (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) in their color.
  3. Place the 1-Point and 3-Point Prestige VP tokens in a central bank.

III. Gameplay Structure (3 Rounds)

The game lasts 3 rounds (investigating all 9 locations). Each round has two phases:

  • Phase 1: Taking Shots: Players take turns placing one Camera Shot Token (1–5) face-down under any active location until everyone has assigned 3 tokens.
  • Phase 2: Exposure Resolution: Reveal all tokens under each location and add their values together.

IV. Resolving Locations

  • Below Min Requirement: Image is too dark or blurry. The story cannot be printed!
  • Sweet Spot (Min to Bust Limit - 1): SCOOP PUBLISHED!
    • Highest single Shot Token wins 1st Place Scoop Points.
    • 2nd highest single Shot Token wins 2nd Place Scoop Points.
  • At or Above Bust Limit: MYTH BUSTED! The shot was over-exposed, proving the cryptid is just a tree trunk, owl, or draft!
    • The single highest contributor loses -2 Prestige VP.

V. Tie-Breakers & Victory

If players tie for the highest single token, the player who placed their token earliest wins priority (unless a Role modifier applies).

After all 3 rounds (9 total locations), the journalist with the most Prestige Victory Points wins!
