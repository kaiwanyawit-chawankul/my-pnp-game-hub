9 CARDS LAKE FISHINGTHE GAME

Welcome to the lake

You are an elite angler competing on a shared lake. Manage your bait, judge the distance, and spend power to roll dice from one shared pool. Reel in fish from the shallows and the deep, then face the legendary Kingfish.

How to win Collect the most Victory Points (VP). The game ends the instant someone lands the Kingfish.

9 CARDS LAKE FISHINGSETUP

Setup

  1. 1
    Place the Tracking Card beside the play area, within reach of both anglers. It has a Power row for each angler and the Kingfish HP row.
  2. 2
    Power: put each angler’s Strength Token on space 3 of their row.
  3. 3
    Kingfish HP: put the Kingfish Token at 15 (standard) or 20 (2-Player Balanced Variant).
  4. 4
    Supplies: each angler takes 1 Red, 1 Blue and 1 Green Bait Token plus 1 Score Token.
  5. 5
    Build the Lake: shuffle the 7 regular fish. Deal 4 face-down as the top layer (positions 1–4). Shuffle the other 3 with the Kingfish and put them underneath (positions 5–8). Fan the pile so every card’s back shows. The card nearest you is distance 1.

9 CARDS LAKE FISHINGCORE IDEAS

Three ideas run the whole game

🎲

Shared Dice Pool

All 7 dice are shared. Every distance roll, spot roll-off and catch roll uses dice from this one pool.

Dice go back to the pool as soon as the catch is resolved or the trackers are updated.

💪

Power Level

Your Strength Token on the 1–5 scale. It starts on 3.

Spend 1 ↓ = +1 extra die on any roll-off or catch roll.

Bank ↑ unused dice = +1 each, up to 5.

🎣

Bait

You place Red, Blue and Green tokens on cards in the Lake Deck.

Catch it: bait returns free.

Anything else: pay 1 Power to get it back.

9 CARDS LAKE FISHINGTURN: PHASES 1–3

1Roll & Distance

  1. Every angler rolls 1 die from the pool, all at the same time.
  2. Pick a target card 1 to 6 positions down the Lake Deck.
  3. Roll − Distance = Power Dice
    That is how many dice you can roll this turn.
  4. Banking: you may roll fewer dice and bank the rest: +1 Power Level per banked die (max 5).
Example A · Roll and bank
Roll 6, target distance 1: 6 − 1 = 5 dice. Roll 3 for the catch, bank 2: Power +2.
Example B · Short roll
Roll 1, distance 1: 1 − 1 = 0 dice. Spend 1 Power (3 → 2) to draw 1 extra die for the catch.

2Place Bait

All anglers place bait at the same time. Decide first, then put your tokens down together so nobody can copy. Pick tokens from your supply and put them on your target card. Spread your 3 baits over 3 spots, or stack up to 3 on one spot to go all-in on a valuable fish.

3Spot Competition

Look at what everyone placed. If bait from several anglers sits on the same card, one angler gets priority to catch it:

① Bait match

Bait that matches the fish wins over non-matching bait. No roll needed.

② Roll-off

Both match or both miss? Each rolls dice from the pool. The higher total wins.
Power option: any competitor may spend 1 Power (↓ 1 space) to add +1 die to their roll-off.
Losing a spot: you make no catch this round. Bank your unused power dice and take your bait back (1 Power per token).
Different cards? Everyone still catches, one at a time. A spot winner goes first; otherwise the order alternates each round (whoever caught second last round goes first).

9 CARDS LAKE FISHINGTURN: PHASES 4–5

4Catch

One by oneAnglers now catch one at a time in catch order (a spot winner goes first). Each reveals their card, then rolls their power dice.

Bait matches

Target = Fish Power

Bait does not match

Target = Fish Power + 1

Your dice total must be strictly more than the target.

✔ Success

Keep the card. Your bait returns free. Banked dice raise your Power Level; the rest return to the pool.

✘ Failure

The fish flees face-down to the bottom of the Lake Deck. Spend 1 Power to recover your bait.

5Kingfish Encounter

When the Kingfish is revealed it is too strong to land in one roll. Use the same target as above (matching bait: its Power; otherwise Power + 1).

Damage

Damage = Dice Total − Target
Roll beats the target? Move the Kingfish Token down by the damage.

Landing it

At 0 HP you claim the Kingfish and the game ends at once. Above 0, it stays face up at the bottom and play passes on.

Game end & scoring

The game ends immediately in Phase 5 when someone drops the Kingfish to 0 HP and lands it.

Total Score = Regular Fish VP + Kingfish Card VP + 2-Player Bonus

Count

① VP of your regular fish
② VP on the Kingfish card (if yours)
③ +5 VP if you landed it in a 2-player game

Winner

The highest total is Master Angler.
Tie? The angler who landed the Kingfish wins.
