Strategies and rule assumptions

    Assumptions (the rulebook is silent on these): the second-placed angler is not visible, so choices are made blind. Same card chosen by both = spot contest: a bait match wins, otherwise each rolls 1 die + 1 die per Power spent, ties go to the angler first in catch order. The loser makes no catch, banks unused dice and pays 1 Power per bait token to retrieve. Otherwise catches are one by one and the order alternates each round. Failure or retrieval costs 1 Power per token (floor 1; unpaid tokens stay stuck). Players see only the 3 card-back sizes (S/M/L: Ayu+Bass, Herabuna+Magoi, Large = Biwamasu, Namazu, Ito, Kingfish). The Kingfish accepts any bait.
