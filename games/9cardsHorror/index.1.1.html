A hidden-role chase for 2–5 players, about 15 minutes
THE NIGHT SHIFT

Nobody goes down the Hollow Mine unless they have to. You did. The rent was late, the debts were older, and the company paid triple for one night of ’clearing work’. The foreman gave each of you two candles, locked the gate behind you and told you not to answer the knocking. Now the old caretaker is awake in his room at the back of the tunnels, and he is coming for you, one slow step at a time. The only key to the gate is somewhere down here.

And one of you was paid extra, up front, to make sure that not everyone comes back up.

The game

A killer is walking the tunnel from left to right. Find the Key, reach the Exit and get out. But one of you may be a Betrayer who wants someone to die.

You need: these 9 cards, the tokens (page 4), and a six-sided die.

Setup

  • Put the Caretaker’s Room (the Key card) face-down on the far left and stand the Killer (normal side up) on it. Put the Exit on the far right.
  • Shuffle the other 7 scene cards. Lay 3 in a row between the Key card and the Exit. The other 4 form the deck.
  • Flip the first card of the row face-up. All pawns start on it, front side up (ignore its text this once). Because the Killer sits on the Key card, the Key can’t turn up in the row too early.
  • Each player takes 2 candles.
  • Pawns and roles: the pawns look identical on the front; the back shows the role. Take the Betrayer pawn plus 1 Survivor pawn for every other player, shuffle them face-down, and everyone draws one and peeks at the back. Exactly one Betrayer. With 2 players, use 2 Survivor pawns.

A round

Players take turns. On your turn you get 2 actions (you may repeat one). When everyone has played, the Killer acts.

  • Move 1 card left or right. If the card is face-down, flip it and follow its text.
  • Use the ACTION printed on your card (search, peek...).
  • Give any number of tokens, and/or the Key, to one player on your card (1 action), or pick up a dropped Key.
  • Unlock or Escape at the Exit (see Winning).

Pooling: players on the same card may combine tokens (any mix) to pay one cost: searching, peeking, a dead end, Break Free, Footsteps. Surviving a kill is personal: only the victim pays, so give tokens first if you want to help.

The Killer (end of every round)

  • If players share his card, he kills one of them and stays put.
  • Otherwise he moves 1 card right. At the end of round 1 he steps off the Key card onto the first card of the row. If players are on the new card, he kills one at once.
  • The card he leaves is removed to a face-up pile. The exception is the Key card: while its Key is untaken, shuffle it face-down into the deck instead. A Key lying on a card he leaves moves onto the Killer’s card.
  • Who dies? Everyone on the card rolls the die. The lowest roll is the victim. On a tie, all tied players are victims. A lone player is the victim automatically.
  • Fight back: a victim may spend 1 candle to survive this round, or 1 flashlight to survive and stun the Killer (flip him: he does nothing at the end of the next round, then flips back).
  • A victim who doesn’t survive becomes a ghost: take your pawn off the board and keep it face-down in front of you (your role still counts), drop the Key (if carried) on that card and discard all other tokens. Hand them over before it happens!

Run! If anyone is still on his card next round, he kills again.

Candles and the lighter

  • Lighting: every time you spend a candle, roll the die. On 3+ it lights and the cost is paid. On 1–2 it sputters: nothing happens, but you keep the candle. Flashlights never need a roll.
  • The lighter (found on the Dead Miner): its holder’s candles always light, no roll. Pass it on with Give.
  • Burn the Killer: the holder may spend 1 action and roll. On 4+ the Killer is stunned, like with a flashlight. On a 1 the lighter runs empty: flip it, it is useless from now on.

Finding the Key

  • Search: in the Caretaker’s Room, spend 2 candles and/or flashlights (any mix) to take the Key.
  • Dead end: if you stand on the last card before the Exit and the Key card hasn’t been revealed, spend 1 candle or 1 flashlight to add the top deck card to the row, face-down, just left of the Exit.
  • ONE USE cards: turn them sideways once used.

Ghosts

A ghost keeps its secret role (it still counts) and stays in the game. It cannot move, carry tokens or be killed again. On its turn it may whisper advice to the group (it may lie). Once per game it may secretly look at the top 2 cards of the deck and swap them, or leave them.

Winning

  • Unlock: a player on the Exit with the Key spends 1 action. The door stays open: any player on the Exit may then spend 1 action to escape.
  • Survivors win if at least one Survivor escapes.
  • The Betrayer wins if no Survivor escapes (even if he escapes himself, or is dead).
  • If nobody escapes, the Killer and the Betrayer win. The game ends when every player has escaped or become a ghost. Reveal the pawns.

Bluff freely. You may say anything about your role, your tokens or the cards you have peeked at.

TOKENS AT A GLANCE

Candle: survive a kill for one round. Peek (Dark Passage). Break free (Narrow Crawl). Roll 3+ to light it (no roll for the lighter’s holder).

Flashlight: survive a kill and stun the Killer. Ignore Wrong Turn.

Candle or flashlight: search the Caretaker’s Room (2 of them). Add a card at a dead end (1). Cancel the Footsteps extra step (1).

Key: found in the Caretaker’s Room. Carry it to the Exit. If its carrier dies it drops on that space. If the Killer passes it, it moves onto his space.

Lighter: found on the Dead Miner. Its holder’s candles always light. Burn the Killer: 1 action, roll 4+ to stun him; on a 1 it is empty.

Pooling: players on the same card can chip in tokens together to pay one cost. A victim pays alone to survive a kill.

Stun: flip the Killer to his stunned side. At the end of the next round he does nothing (no move, no kill), then flips back.

Start: every player has 2 candles, no flashlights, no lighter, no Key.
