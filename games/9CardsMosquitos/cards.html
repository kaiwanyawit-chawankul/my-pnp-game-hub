Quick Reference

  1. Placement: Deploy 2 tokens (All-In) OR 1 token (Hold Reserve).
  2. Roll Slap Dice:
    • 1: Safe
    • 2-4: Neck/Arms/Legs
    • 5: Heavy Target (Tie Safe)
    • 6: Frenzy (Tie Danger)
  3. Late Stinger: On 5 or 6, reserve tokens can be placed to manipulate ties.
  4. Feeding: Surviving Normal = +2 pts. Reserve = +1 pt. First to 10 wins!
