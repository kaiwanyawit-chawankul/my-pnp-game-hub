# MOSQUITO BITES
*A fast-paced push-your-luck & take-that card game for 1–5 Players*

## OVERVIEW
You are a gang of hungry mosquitos attempting to drain blood from a human host without getting swatted. Deploy your swarm strategically, use reserves to manipulate where the host slaps, and be the first gang to reach **10 Blood Points**!

---

## COMPONENTS
* 3 Body Location Cards (Neck, Body, Legs)
* 1 Slap Dice (6-sided)
* Per Player (Up to 5 players):
  * 1 Mosquito Tracking Card
  * 1 Blood Tracker Token
  * 2 Mosquito Tokens

---

## SETUP
1. Place the 3 Body Location Cards (**Neck**, **Body**, **Legs**) face-up in the center of the table.
2. Each player takes 1 Mosquito Tracking Card, 1 Blood Tracker Token (placed at **0**), and 2 Mosquito Tokens.

---

## GAMEPLAY
The game is played in rounds until a player reaches **10 Blood Points**. Each round consists of 4 steps:

### Step 1: Placement & Reserve
Simultaneously (or in turn order), each player chooses how to deploy their 2 Mosquito Tokens:
* **All-In (Normal Stinger)**: Place **both Mosquito Tokens** across the 3 Body Location Cards (2 points per surviving token).
* **Hold in Reserve**: Place **1 Mosquito Token** on a Body Location Card and keep **1 token in your hand** (Reserve).

---

### Step 2: Roll the Slap Dice
One player rolls the Slap Dice:

| Dice Roll | Result | Effect |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **No Slap** | Safe! No location is slapped. |
| **2** | **Slap Neck** | The **Neck** card is slapped. |
| **3** | **Slap Body** | The **Body** card is slapped. |
| **4** | **Slap Legs** | The **Legs** card is slapped. |
| **5** | **Heavy Target (Tie Safe)** | Slaps the location with the **MOST** mosquitos. *If tied for most, NO location is slapped.* |
| **6** | **Frenzy Slap (Tie Danger)** | Slaps the location with the **MOST** mosquitos. *If tied for most, ALL tied locations are slapped!* |

---

### Step 3: Late Stinger Action (Sabotage / Shielding)
If **Dice 5 or Dice 6** is rolled, any player who kept a Mosquito Token in **Reserve** may now place their reserved token onto any location **before** the slap resolves:

* **Forcing a Tie-Safe (on Dice 5)**: Place your reserved token on a lower-count location to tie the highest location. Because Dice 5 safe-guards ties, **NO location gets slapped!**
* **Triggering a Frenzy (on Dice 6)**: Place your reserved token on an opponent’s crowded spot to tie it with another location. On Dice 6, **BOTH locations get wiped out!**

*(If multiple players want to use Late Stinger, resolve in clockwise order starting from the dice roller.)*

---

### Step 4: Slap Resolution & Feeding
1. **Swatting**: All Mosquito Tokens on a slapped location are squished for the round and collect no blood.
2. **Normal Stinger Feeding**: Each surviving token placed during **Step 1** scores **+2 Blood Points**.
3. **Late Stinger Feeding**: Each surviving token placed during **Step 3 (Reserve)** scores **+1 Blood Point**.
4. Advance your Blood Tracker and return all tokens to players' hands.

---

## END OF GAME & WINNING
The game ends immediately at the end of any round in which one or more players reach **10 Blood Points**. The player with the highest score wins!

### Tie-Breakers:
1. **Most Active Mosquitos**: The tied player with the most surviving Mosquito Tokens in the final round.
2. **Mosquito Attractor / Repellent**: The player who **got bit by a real mosquito most recently** OR is **currently holding mosquito repellent** wins!
