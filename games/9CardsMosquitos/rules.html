Mosquito Bites

A fast-paced push-your-luck & take-that card game for 1–5 Players

Overview

You are a gang of hungry mosquitos attempting to drain blood from a human host without getting swatted. Deploy your swarm strategically, use reserves to manipulate where the host slaps, and be the first gang to reach 10 Blood Points!

Components

  • 3 Body Location Cards (Neck, Arms, Legs)
  • 1 Slap Dice (6-sided)
  • Per Player (Up to 5 players):
    • 1 Mosquito Tracking Card
    • 1 Blood Tracker Token
    • 2 Mosquito Tokens

Setup

  1. Place the 3 Body Location Cards (Neck, Arms, Legs) face-up in the center of the table.
  2. Each player takes 1 Mosquito Tracking Card, 1 Blood Tracker Token (placed at 0), and 2 Mosquito Tokens.

Gameplay

The game is played in rounds until a player reaches 10 Blood Points. Each round consists of 4 steps:

Step 1: Placement & Reserve

Simultaneously (or in turn order), each player chooses how to deploy their 2 Mosquito Tokens:

  • All-In (Normal Stinger): Place both Mosquito Tokens across the 3 Body Location Cards (2 points per surviving token).
  • Hold in Reserve: Place 1 Mosquito Token on a Body Location Card and keep 1 token in your hand (Reserve).

Step 2: Roll the Slap Dice

One player rolls the Slap Dice:

Roll Result Effect
1 No Slap Safe! No location is slapped.
2 Slap Neck The Neck card is slapped.
3 Slap Arms The Arms card is slapped.
4 Slap Legs The Legs card is slapped.
5 Heavy Target (Tie Safe) Slaps location with MOST mosquitos. If tied, NO location is slapped.
6 Frenzy Slap (Tie Danger) Slaps location with MOST mosquitos. If tied, ALL tied locations slapped!

Step 3: Late Stinger Action

If Dice 5 or Dice 6 is rolled, any player who kept a token in Reserve may place it onto any location before the slap resolves:

  • Forcing a Tie-Safe (Dice 5): Tie the highest location. NO location gets slapped!
  • Triggering a Frenzy (Dice 6): Tie it with another location. BOTH locations get wiped out!

(Resolve in clockwise order starting from the dice roller.)

Step 4: Slap Resolution & Feeding

  1. Swatting: Slapped tokens are squished and collect no blood.
  2. Normal Stinger: Each surviving token from Step 1 scores +2 Blood Points.
  3. Late Stinger: Each surviving token from Step 3 scores +1 Blood Point.
  4. Advance Blood Tracker and return all tokens to hands.

End of Game & Winning

The game ends immediately at the end of any round in which one or more players reach 10 Blood Points. The player with the highest score wins!

Tie-Breakers:

  1. Most Active Mosquitos: The tied player with the most surviving Mosquito Tokens in the final round.
  2. Mosquito Attractor / Repellent: The player who got bit by a real mosquito most recently OR is currently holding mosquito repellent wins!
